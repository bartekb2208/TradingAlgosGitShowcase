# Disclaimer

**This repository is a showcase.** It's a curated, read-only snapshot of a private research
repository, published so that people evaluating my work (employers, mainly) can see how I
approach systematic trading research and the engineering around it.

## Not financial advice

Nothing in this repository is financial, investment or trading advice, or a recommendation to buy,
sell or hold anything. I'm not a licensed financial adviser. Backtest results are hypothetical,
are produced on historical data with modelled costs, and do not predict future results.

## Not for live trading

**Do not run any of this code against a live or funded account.** Most of the strategies here
failed their own tests, which is why their code is published. Several were never tested at all.
The code is reproduced as it was written during research, including known defects, obsolete API
calls and assumptions about a specific broker's instruments. Trading leveraged products such as
CFDs and futures can lose more than you deposit.

## Not the complete picture

- The three strategies that survived testing are described by process and results only. **Their
  source code and parameters aren't here**, and nothing in this repository is enough to trade them.
- No market data is included. Databento market-by-order data is licensed and can't be
  redistributed; broker exports belong to the broker.
- Third-party documents the research drew on (a trading-course PDF, an academic paper, a book) are
  cited, not included.
- This repository was created fresh, with no history from the private repository.
