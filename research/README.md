## About the documents

The pre-registrations, design specs and results files are **working notes copied from the private
repository**, edited only to remove a prop-firm name, point file paths at this repository, cite
documents that can't be published instead of linking them, and paraphrase passages quoted from a
third-party source. Everything else is as it was written at the time, including the mistakes they
record.

Two consequences:

- **File paths inside them point at this repository.** Each study's outputs live in its
  `artifacts/` folder, and market data is expected under `data/`, which is not included.
- **Some notes refer to me in the third person** ("the user", "Bartek"). They were written with an
  AI coding assistant during the work, and are reproduced as written rather than tidied up after
  the fact.

The `README.md` in each folder and the three survivor write-ups were written for this showcase.
